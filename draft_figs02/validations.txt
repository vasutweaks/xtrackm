Validation scatter plots comparing altimetry-derived zonal currents with independent observations from RAMA buoys, OMNI currents, and surface drifters. Left-column panels show comparisons without Ekman correction, and right-column panels show comparisons after adding Ekman zonal velocity to the altimetry current. Each subplot reports the number of matched points, correlation, standard error, and linear-fit equation. The red line is the linear fit, the white dashed line is the 1:1 reference, and colors indicate point density.
