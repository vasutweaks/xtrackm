a) Scatter plot of drifters' zonal velocities versus altimetry derived velocities within 200km inshore of the coast for the satellite mission TP+J1+J2+J3+S6A. b) shows scatter plot with altimetry derived velocities corrected with ekman zonal velocities.The colorbar shows density of points in the scatter plot in terms of count.
