Fig. X. Long-term variability and linear trends of geostrophic current speed at seven crossover points of the East India Coastal Current (EICC) derived from ERS-1/2, Envisat, and SARAL/AltiKa altimetry. (Left) Locations of the seven crossover points (A–G, south to north) along the eastern boundary of the Bay of Bengal (10°–22°N, 78°–88°E). Each point is colour-coded by its label; the annotated value is the linear trend in m s⁻¹ yr⁻¹ (greyed out where the trend is not significant at the 95% confidence level, p ≥ 0.05). (Right) 500-day low-pass filtered current speed (coloured line) and its linear trend fit (black dashed line) at each point. Background shading indicates the spring inter-monsoon (February–May, yellow), summer monsoon (June–September, cyan), and post-monsoon/early winter (October–December, grey) seasons. Point coordinates and contributing ground-track pairs are: A (80.26°E, 11.30°N; T868×T567), B (80.62°E, 12.89°N; T868×T23), C (80.98°E, 14.46°N; T868×T481), D (82.06°E, 16.01°N; T324×T395), E (83.86°E, 17.55°N; T238×T767), F (85.65°E, 19.07°N; T152×T137), and G (87.45°E, 20.57°N; T66×T509).
