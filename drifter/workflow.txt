c.close.drifters_at_intersection_point*.py

The purpose of above scripts is to find for each intersection point of
satellite tracks, we find a list of drifter ids that are passing through
a small box around the intersection point. So for every intersection point we
will have a list of drifters going closeby to the intersection points.
