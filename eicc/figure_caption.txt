Figure X. Regional context and low-pass filtered variability of Bay of Bengal and equatorial Indian Ocean forcing/response. Left panel shows the analysis masks over the Indian Ocean: equatorial band (5°S-5°N, green hatch), Bay of Bengal gyre region (blue hatch), and Bay of Bengal along-shore region (red hatch). Right panels (a-d) show monthly area-averaged time series after applying a 500-day low-pass filter, with linear trend lines (black dashed): (a) Ekman pumping over the Bay of Bengal gyre mask, (b) sea-level anomaly over the Bay of Bengal gyre mask, (c) windstress magnitude over the Bay of Bengal along-shore mask, and (d) windstress magnitude over the equatorial band. Spatial means are cosine-latitude weighted, and Bay of Bengal masks are interpolated to each dataset grid before averaging.
