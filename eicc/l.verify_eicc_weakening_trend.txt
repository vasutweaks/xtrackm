VERIFICATION OF THE MULTIDECADAL EICC WEAKENING CLAIM
draft_v02.2a, Figure 6 / p.speed_at_coastal_points.eicc.trends.all_in_one.ERS.py

CLAIM UNDER TEST
    Track-derived speed at coastal intersection points A-G, sat =
    ERS1+ERS2+ENV+SRL, gives a negative trend at all 7 points, all flagged
    significant, mean -32% over 30 years. The draft states 55% weakening at
    point B and ~30% averaged over all points.

BOTTOM LINE
    The -30% weakening does not survive verification. It is almost certainly
    an instrument artifact: a step change in noise level at the ERS-2/ENVISAT
    transition, rectified into the positive-definite speed |V| through an
    ill-conditioned two-track inversion. The disagreement with OSCAR, GLORYS,
    ECCO and AVISO is the symptom, not a puzzle to be explained away.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE TREND LIVES ENTIRELY AT THE ERS-2 -> ENVISAT BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    mean over A-G:
        full-record trend of |V|                     -0.0098 m/s/yr
        pooled WITHIN-era trend                      -0.0001 m/s/yr
          (ERS-1+2 alone, and ENV+SRL alone)
        step in mean |V| across mid-2002             -0.155  m/s

    Inside each instrument era there is no trend at all, and the within-era
    signs are mixed - A, C, D, E are all POSITIVE in the ENV+SRL half. The
    whole -30% is one step at the mission change.

     pt  full record   ERS-1+2   ENV+SRL  mean early  mean late    step
      A      -0.0176   -0.0055   +0.0187       1.108      0.778  -0.330
      B      -0.0231   -0.0307   -0.0138       0.971      0.704  -0.267
      C      -0.0045   +0.0419   +0.0092       0.798      0.637  -0.161

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE STEP IS EXACTLY THE DROP IN NOISE LEVEL (RICE RECTIFICATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    |V| is positive-definite, so it rectifies noise:
        E[|V|] ~ sqrt( |V_true|^2 + sigma^2 )

    Across the same mission boundary:
        mean cycle-to-cycle noise ratio (late/early)      0.843
        mean speed ratio            (late/early)          0.827
        correlation of the two ratios across the 7 points 0.62

    ENVISAT and SARAL/AltiKa are quieter instruments and retrack much better
    near the coast - valid-cycle return goes 52% (ERS-1 era) -> 95% (SARAL).
    The speed drop tracks the noise drop almost exactly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE TWO-TRACK INVERSION IS BADLY CONDITIONED, SO NOISE DOMINATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Every ERS crossing at these points is at ~25 degrees. That amplifies
    error into gv: std(gv)/std(gu) reaches 5.4 at point A, with
    std(gv) = 1.16 m/s - larger than any real EICC variability.

    Signal-to-noise of |V|:
        pt    A     B     C     D     E     F     G
        SNR  0.64  0.80  0.53  0.38  0.61  0.46  0.67

    Noise exceeds signal at EVERY point. Consistently, the mean speeds in
    Figure 6 (0.47-0.92 m/s) are 2-3x AVISO's at the same locations
    (0.17-0.39 m/s) - inflated, not measured.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SIGNED COMPONENTS SHOW NO TREND; AVISO OVER THE SAME PERIOD SHOWS NO TREND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    The signed gu/gv trends are insignificant almost everywhere, and where
    significant they disagree in sign (A +0.006, B -0.006 m/s/yr).

    Period note: the guv files end 2016-04, not 2023. The record is 23.4
    years, so "three decades" and the %/30y figures are extrapolations.
    1994, 2011 and 2012 are entirely absent from the record.

    Matching AVISO to the actual record period, same points, same 2002
    boundary:
        AVISO   1993-2016:  +0.0008 m/s/yr,  step +0.007 m/s
        X-TRACK 1993-2016:  -0.0098 m/s/yr,  step -0.155 m/s

    Same quantity, same points, same period. Only the along-track record
    steps.

    Side note: the Ekman term that Figure 6 scalar-adds contributes
    -0.0002 m/s/yr, about 1% of the claimed trend. Adding speeds rather than
    vectors is wrong, but it is not what drives this.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO VERIFY - RECOMMENDED PATH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Use the signed single-track cross-track current gc, not |V|.
   At these near-perpendicular coastal crossings gc is essentially the
   alongshore EICC, needs no ill-conditioned inversion, and being signed it
   does not rectify noise. Its trends are insignificant at 6 of 7 points.

2. Use a noise-immune strength metric: the annual-harmonic amplitude of the
   signed gc. Noise does not project onto the annual harmonic.
   Computed (1993-2002 vs 2003-2016):

        A -37%   B +50%   C -87%   D +9%   E -34%   F +9%   G -12%

   Mixed signs, no coherent basin-wide weakening. This is the honest result
   from the robust metric.

3. If a trend is still wanted: fit trend + mission-step jointly and report
   both; estimate inter-mission offsets in the ERS-2/ENVISAT overlap.

4. Cross-check against a single-mission record. TP+J1+J2+J3+S6A spans
   1992-2023 continuously on one orbit family and would not carry this step.

FALSIFICATION TEST
    If the ERS-2/ENVISAT step also appears in TP+J1+J2+J3+S6A at the same
    points and the same dates, it is oceanic rather than instrumental and
    this diagnosis is wrong. That test is cheap and should be run before
    revising the draft text.
