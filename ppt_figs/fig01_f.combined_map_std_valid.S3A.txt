Comparison of Sentinel-3A (S3A) sea level anomaly (SLA) data between X-TRACK and along-track Level-2+ (L2P) products for common ground tracks that are meeting the east coast of India. (a) Selected common S3A tracks plotted (black) over ETOPO5 bathymetry, with track numbers annotated in white. (b) Percentage of valid SLA observations available along-track plotted against GSHHG coastline distance, showing X-TRACK (blue) and L2P (red) individual track values (dots) and multi-track means (solid lines). The dashed vertical line in (b) and (c) marks the coastline. (c) Temporal standard deviation of SLA as a function of distance from coast for individual tracks (dots) and their multi-track mean (solid lines) from X-TRACK (blue) and L2P (red) products.
