WHAT THIS IS
    A simplified, verified rewrite of the X-TRACK along-track library
    (lib/tools_xtrackm.py, ~2300 lines) into lib/tools_xtrackm_c.py, plus a
    review of the paper figures that depend on it. tools_xtrackm.py has NOT
    been modified: nothing that imports it has changed behaviour.

================================================================================
A.  THE THREE THINGS THAT MATTER MOST
================================================================================

A1. THE PAPER HAS A SIGN ERROR. draft_v02.2a writes
        v = (gc2 sin a1 - gc1 sin a2) / sin(a2 - a1)
    The exact inverse of its OWN gc1/gc2 equations has the numerator the other
    way round. Over 18039 random test cases the draft's u recovers the truth
    100% of the time and the draft's v 0% of the time. The gc definition and
    the projection equations are fine; only the v inversion is wrong.
    -> FIX THE PAPER. (section 8.2)

A2. THE CODE IS CORRECT BY ACCIDENT. compute_geostrophy_gc and
    geostrophic_components each carry a sign error and the two cancel exactly.
    Fixing either one alone flips the answer. Verified end to end against a
    synthetic uniform current: truth (0.4000, 0.1500), library chain
    (0.4000, 0.1501).
    -> DO NOT "FIX" ONE OF THEM IN ISOLATION. (section 8.1)

A3. SEVEN ANALYSIS SCRIPTS COMPUTE u WITH THE WRONG SIGN. CLAUDE.md says the 15
    scripts with a local geostrophic_components_from_a "are equivalent" to the
    library function. They are not -- all 15 implement the DRAFT's formulas, and
    8 patch u by hand with "u = -1*u". The 7 without the patch give -u.
    guv/p.aviso_only_uvVStrack_guv.at.intersections_single.py has the line
    "u = u" where the others have "u = -1*u" -- the patch was deleted and a
    no-op left behind. That is the AVISO script, and guv/Notes.txt records
    exactly the symptom this produces ("I expected correlations of above 0.9
    with aviso guv ... but it was not the case").
    -> FIX THE "u = u" NO-OP AND RE-RUN. (section 8.6, full file list there)

================================================================================
B.  THE NEW LIBRARY  (lib/tools_xtrackm_c.py, 17 exports)
================================================================================

RESHAPING
    track_dist_time_asn_midx(ds, var_str, units_in, dist_from)
        (x, time) with lat/lon carrying their own indexes, so .sel works on
        distance, time, latitude and longitude alike. Handles 1-D variables.
        Collapses 6 old functions into one.
    along_track_dist, track_time, sel_track

GEOMETRY
    local_angle(lons, lats, ii, ioff, degrees)     geodesic, NaN-safe
    track_angle_at(ds, lat=|lon=|x=, order=)       index cannot be mismatched

GEOSTROPHY   (convention stated once: alpha = offshore heading,
              gc = -u sin a + v cos a = +(g/f) dh/dx)
    coriolis, gravity, OMEGA
    cross_track_geostrophy(da, equator_band=5.0, max_gap=None)
    resolve_uv(gc1, gc2, a1, a2, min_sin=0.1)

FILTERING    (two rules: never invent data, never silently destroy it)
    mad_filter(da, thresh=3.5, dim=None)
    smooth_box / smooth_gaussian / smooth_loess
    lowpass_time(da, cutoff_days, order, dim)      sampling read from the axis

Run `python tools_xtrackm_c.py` for 11 groups of usage examples and 5 figures.

================================================================================
C.  DEFECTS FOUND, RANKED BY IMPACT
================================================================================

FIXED IN THE NEW LIBRARY
  mad_filter_1d_xr is 0.6745x TOO LENIENT (31 call sites). scale='normal'
      already divides by 0.6745 and the code divides again, so a nominal 3.5
      cuts at 5.19 sigma. On GFO 008 it flagged 2 outliers where 110 was right.
      Outlier rejection across the project has been near a no-op.   (9.1)
  THE LOESS SMOOTHERS FABRICATE DATA. interpolate_na(..., "extrapolate") then
      kept everywhere: 28695 valid points in, 32550 out -- 3855 invented, and at
      x=0 (the coast) 37 valid cycles became 155. The fabricated points also
      corrupt the fit at genuine observations (median 0.20 cm, max 86 cm; worst
      in the 50-150 km band the paper's argument lives in). track_smooth_loess_x
      additionally RAISES LinAlgError on GFO 008.                    (9.3)
  butter_lowpass_1d/2d USE THE WRONG SAMPLING RATE (20 call sites). fs is
      hardcoded at 1 sample/day; the series is one sample per repeat cycle, so a
      "3-day" cutoff is 30 d for Jason, 51 for GFO, 105 for ERS. On 6-hourly
      drifter data the same call gives 18 HOURS.                (9.2, 14.2)
  track_smooth_gaussian_x DESTROYS 59% OF THE DATA (28695 -> 11865): one NaN
      blanks the whole kernel. Fixed by normalised convolution.       (9.4a)
  track_smooth_box_x DEFAULTS TO smooth=1 -- a no-op.                 (9.4b)
  local_angle WAS FLAT-EARTH (mean 0.26 deg, max 2.03 deg error) AND CALLED
      WITH A MIRRORED INDEX. index_at_lat returns a REVERSED-array index and
      every call site paired it with FORWARD arrays, so the heading came from
      the mirror-image point: mean 0.78 deg error, up to 53 deg. New geodesic
      version is accurate to 0.004 deg; track_angle_at removes the trap. (7)
  compute_geostrophy_gc: one-sided lat > 2 mask (draft says 5S-5N); f and g
      aligned by array POSITION not coordinate; no x-unit check (a km axis
      gives 427 m/s silently); differentiate straddles gaps up to 1384 km;
      near-parallel threshold 1e-6 admits crossings of 6e-5 deg.      (8.3)

FOUND, NOT PORTED
  geostrophic_current_components_1 has an operator-precedence bug:
      "/ 2*cos_theta" is (x/2)*cos, not x/(2 cos) -- 1.333x off at 30 deg.
  compute_geostrophy_from_slope uses an endpoint-to-endpoint slope and
      abs(angle), destroying the sign for descending tracks.
  slope_smooth_optimal_filter_x uses np.convolve, which reverses an asymmetric
      kernel. (genweights itself is fine.)                        (8.3g,h, 9.4c)

DATA / PROVENANCE TRAPS
  guv/guv_at_intersections_loess/gc_at_*.nc ARE UNUSABLE. The name carries no
      intersection identifier but a track is "self" in many pairs (track 522 in
      10). Each pair overwrites the file; the one on disk for 522 matches
      x = 1263.7 km (corr +0.9968), not the 571.5 km of the 522x279 pair. No
      script reads them, so it is latent -- but it defeats any rebuild.  (11.2)
  compute_dist ASSUMES THE LAST POINT IS COASTAL. For 32 tracks the FIRST point
      is nearer (ERS 666: 7.4 km vs 105.6 km). gc is unaffected but "distance
      from coast" is measured from the wrong end.                      (8.3j)
  THE EKMAN SCRIPT NO LONGER REPRODUCES THE PRODUCT IN USE. A `break` limits it
      to z=0 and a "_bob" subset was added, while xtrackm reads the full-IOD
      files from an earlier run. Not blocking -- all three depths exist. (12.4)
  THE EKMAN PRODUCT IS 180 deg WRONG IN THE SOUTHERN HEMISPHERE (+135 where
      +45 is right; signed f in the prefactor). IRRELEVANT HERE -- 0 of 1418
      NINDIAN tracks reach south of the equator. Left unfixed by decision. (12.2)

D.  THE FIGURE REVIEWS  (draft_figs02 -> draft_figs03, "_c" suffix)
================================================================================

fig01  SLA std and valid points vs distance, X-TRACK vs L2P            (10)
  Panel (b) drew the SCATTER on GSHHG distance and the MEAN LINE on geodesic
  distance in one axis -- they differ by a median 10 km at 50-100 km. It also
  compared RAW COUNTS under a "Percentage" label while X-TRACK has 115 cycles
  and L2P 110-112. Corrected, the paper's central claim is far cleaner:
      GSHHG  0-10 km : XTRACK 69.3%  L2P 25.0%   (+44.3 points)
      GSHHG 10-25 km : XTRACK 90.5%  L2P 55.2%   (+35.3 points)
      converging to identical by 50-100 km.

fig02  OMNI buoy vs track gu + Ekman at one intersection               (11)
  Buoy taken at 15 m, Ekman added at 0 m, with 15 m files sitting beside them.
  AVISO msla is MONTHLY, not daily -- a 24 h match kept 8 of 62 points.
  raise("...") raises a str. Correlations printed but never drawn.
      track 0.665 | +ekman(0m) 0.725 | +ekman(15m) 0.735 | AVISO monthly 0.678
  With the corrected chain (RECOMPUTE_GUV=True): 0.687 / 0.753 on n 62->52.
  TRACK+EKMAN BEATS THE AVISO MONTHLY PRODUCT against the buoy.

fig03  RAMA and OMNI scatter vs gu (+ Ekman)                           (13)
  Same 0 m / 15 m depth mismatch. At 0 m, adding Ekman makes OMNI's rmse WORSE
  than gu alone (0.198 -> 0.209); at 15 m it improves (-> 0.192). OLS slope is
  attenuated by regression dilution (OMNI 0.400 against RMA 0.687), and the
  reported "Std error" was the residual about the FIT, not about the 1:1 line
  the figure draws. N = 1148 came from 26 intersections and 12 buoys.

fig04  GDP drifter ve vs gu (+ Ekman)                              (14, 15)
  48.5% OF THE PLOTTED POINTS CAME FROM DRIFTERS THAT HAD LOST THEIR DROGUE --
  surface followers, biased downwind by ~1% of wind speed. drogue_lost_date was
  in the files and never read. The "72 hour" filter was an 18 HOUR filter.
      as published  n=328  r 0.728  RMA 1.145  rmse 0.342
      drogued only  n=169  r 0.791  RMA 1.039  rmse 0.299
      + |dt|<=2 d   n= 69  r 0.829  RMA 1.002  rmse 0.272
  Ekman depth is the ONE case that should stay at 0 m: for drifters 0 m beats
  15 m on r, RMA and rmse alike.
  fig04b: all eight missions co-located (15042 rows against 1622). Pooled,
  N=4771 over 8 missions and 704 drifters:
      without Ekman  r 0.685  RMA 1.442  bias +0.065
      with Ekman     r 0.727  RMA 1.218  bias +0.002
  Ekman improves r for EVERY mission. Every mission sits above RMA = 1 --
  drifters carry ageostrophic energy the altimeter cannot see.
  The time-match quality scales with the repeat period (2.5 d for Jason, 8.75 d
  for ERS), so a FIXED time cut is not mission-fair; fig04b cuts at a fraction
  of each mission's own repeat. HY2B is not usable (91 rows, 5 after cuts).

fig05  HF radar vs gc + Ekman along a track                            (16)
  THE FIRST FIGURE WITH NO SIGN OR INDEX ERROR -- verified, rotate_to_cross
  agrees with compute_geostrophy_gc to ratio 1.0002. But `gc + ekman` was
  aligned by luck: it works only because the old code truncates times to whole
  days; with corrected sub-second times the overlap is 0 of 115 and the sum
  silently returns all-NaN. n was never reported (median 25 paired cycles of
  115) and the significance guide was a hard-coded r = 0.7.
      corr 0.75-0.88 within 40 km, ~0.45 by 75 km, Ekman above no-Ekman.
